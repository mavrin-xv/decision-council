# Failure categories

After merging failure reasons, tag each with one category. A category with zero reasons is a blind spot: add one reason or write why it cannot apply.

| Category | Ask | Typical reason (past tense) |
|---|---|---|
| Demand | Did enough of the right people want it? | "Sign-ups came from friends; strangers did not convert." |
| Price and margin | Did the numbers work at real volume? | "Shipping cost doubled and ate the margin on small orders." |
| Channel | Could we reach buyers at a cost we could pay? | "Ad costs per lead were 3 times the plan after week 2." |
| Message | Did buyers understand it in 5 seconds? | "People thought it was a one-off gift, not a subscription." |
| Competition | Did someone respond or already own it? | "The market leader matched the price in a month." |
| Execution capacity | Did the team have the hours and skills? | "The founder spent 60% of the time on support." |
| Operations and suppliers | Did the physical or technical chain hold? | "The packaging supplier missed the date." |
| Cash and timing | Did money last until the result arrived? | "Revenue started in month 4; cash ran out in month 3." |
| Dependency and platform | Did a platform, partner or law change under us? | "The marketplace changed fees mid-season." |
| Measurement | Did we notice the failure in time? | "We only looked at revenue monthly and saw the drop 6 weeks late." |
| People and incentives | Did anyone have a reason not to make it work? | "The sales partner earned more pushing the old product." |
| Customer experience | Did the first use deliver? | "First box arrived damaged for 1 in 8 customers." |

## Seats for independent generation

- **Operator**: runs it daily. Sees capacity, suppliers, tooling, support load.
- **Customer**: sees the message, price, first use, reasons to cancel.
- **Funder**: sees cash, timing, opportunity cost, measurement.

Add a fourth seat for regulated or partner-heavy plans: **Partner or regulator**.
