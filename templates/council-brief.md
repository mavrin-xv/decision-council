# Council brief

Fill this before running `board-of-advisors` or `marketing-council`. Every advisor gets the same brief. The Outsider gets only the Decision line and the Offer line.

**Decision:** <one sentence, phrased as a choice: "Should we X, yes or no?" or "A, B or C?">

**Options on the table:** <max 4>

**Context:**
- What the business is, in one line:
- Numbers that matter (each with its source: export, dashboard, invoice, "my guess"):
- What has been tried before and what happened:
- Constraints (money, hours, deadlines, people):

**Offer / what the buyer sees:** <one paragraph, only for marketing or pricing questions>

**Horizon:** <when the decision must be made, when results are expected>

**What would make this a clear no:** <optional; helps the Contrarian>
