# Council brief: Northwind Roasters price change

**Decision:** Raise the standard coffee subscription from $12 to $18 per month for new customers from May 1. Yes, no, or a different version?

**Context (invented example data):**
- Northwind Roasters is a two-person online coffee roaster. 410 active subscribers.
- Standard plan: 2 bags a month, $12, free shipping. Margin after beans, bags and shipping: about 9%.
- Shipping went from $3.10 to $4.40 per box this year.
- Monthly churn on the standard plan: 6.1% (internal export, last 6 months).
- Trial-to-paid: 24%. Most new subscribers come from Instagram posts and a local market stall.
- Two competitors checked on their pricing pages in March: $15 and $17 for a similar plan.

**Horizon and constraints:** decide by April 10. No ad budget. The founders cannot add hours; any change must not add support load.
