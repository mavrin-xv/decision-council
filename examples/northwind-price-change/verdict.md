# Chairman verdict: Northwind Roasters price change

Written from answers.json, the blind reviews and tally.txt. Invented example.

DECISION:    Do differently. Fix the cost per box first: new customers get 3 bags every 6 weeks for $16 from May 1. Existing subscribers keep $12 until November 1, then move to the new plan.
WHY:         Margin is low because shipping rose from $3.10 to $4.40 per box [INTERNAL], not because the price is low. Fewer boxes per bag fixes the cause. $16 sits between the two competitors ($15, $17) [FACT: their pricing pages, March], where $18 would sit above both with nothing new to show.
MAIN RISK:   Trial-to-paid (24% today) falls and there is no ad budget to refill new signups.
FIRST STEP:  This week, email 10 current subscribers and ask whether 3 bags every 6 weeks would suit them. Quote a second courier before May 1.
KILL IF:     Trial-to-paid below 18% for 2 weeks running after May 1.
SPLIT:       Executor wanted a $16 vs $18 split test. Reviewer R3 showed ~40 trials a month cannot settle that in 4 weeks, so the chairman sided with First Principles. The office plan (Expansionist) is parked, not killed: nobody sized it.
NOT COVERED: Whether the market stall can be a pickup point, which would remove shipping for local customers.
CONFIDENCE:  7/10 (unit-cost numbers are internal and recent; the conversion effect of $16 is a guess)
